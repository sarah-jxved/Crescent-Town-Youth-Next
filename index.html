Our First Initiative

From July 2026 to August 2026, we ran a free, eight-week STEAM summer camp for 69 youth in the Crescent Town community. Every Saturday and Sunday throughout the summer, campers explored science, technology, engineering, arts, and mathematics through interactive lessons and a wide variety of hands-on activities.

2026 Summer STEAM Camp

Learn

Crescent Town Youth Next’s summer camp program was a unique, free opportunity designed for youth in grades 4–8 residing in the Crescent Town community. During each camp session, youth took part in a one-hour STEAM lesson while working through a printed workbook connected to the lesson content. Both the lessons and workbook were created by the founder, Sarah Javed, and vice president & chief operating officer (COO), Rayyan Javed, of Crescent Town Youth Next.

Participate

Throughout the program, workbooks were reviewed and marked by volunteers and camp organizers. To encourage participation and engagement, prizes were awarded at the midway and final points of the program to youth who completed their workbooks in their entirety.

Create

Following each lesson, youth participated in hands-on STEAM activities connected to the day’s learning, including projects they could take home. Highlights included engineering electric cars, creating glitter gel pens, constructing foam gliders, making artificial snow, sewing plushies, exploring virtual reality, and so much more! During every camp session, youth were also provided with snacks, with freezies given at the end of each camp day.

Connect

Throughout the program, youth also had opportunities to socialize and build meaningful connections with one another through free-time activities such as board games, building kits, puzzles, and other interactive games. To celebrate both the first and final days of camp, all youth and parents also enjoyed a pizza party together.

The 2026 Summer STEAM Camp Schedule outlined the activities that took place throughout the program.

Program Details

Who
Grades 4–8

The summer camp was open to youth in grades 4–8 residing in the Crescent Town community.

Cost
Completely free

The summer camp was completely free for all participating youth, with all materials, hands-on activities, workbooks, and food provided at no cost.

Dates
July 4 – August 23

The summer camp included eight weeks of programming during the summer of 2026.

Days
Saturday & Sunday

There were sixteen camp sessions in total across July and August, held every Saturday and Sunday during the camp period.

Time
Mostly 3:00–6:30 PM

All camp days, except for the first and last days, ran from 3:00 PM to 6:30 PM. On the first day of camp, programming ran from 2:00 PM to 8:00 PM, and on the final day of camp, from 3:00 PM to 9:00 PM, to accommodate pizza celebrations alongside scheduled programming and activities.

Location
Community Room 3

The program ran in Community Room 3, located at 3 Massey Square, Crescent Town, Toronto.

Funding Acknowledgement

The 2026 Summer STEAM Camp was generously supported by funding from Canada Service Corps and Trent University's Impact Microgrant Program.

The Difference the Camp Made

Youth and parents were surveyed after the camp ended to assess the summer camp’s impact in more detail.

Among youth who responded:

  • >50%Had never attended a summer camp before ours
  • 92.6%Had never attended a STEAM camp before ours
  • 88.9%Felt more confident trying new activities after participating in our camp
  • 92.6%Said our camp helped them become more creative
  • 96.3%Said they enjoyed participating in our summer camp

Among parents who responded:

  • 100%said their child enjoyed participating in the program
  • 100%said their child learned new skills throughout the program
  • 100%said the program increased their child’s interest in STEAM
  • 100%said the program provided meaningful opportunities for their child to learn and have fun

The Two Youth Behind Crescent Town Youth Next

Sarah Javed

Founder · Crescent Town Youth Next

Hi everyone! My name is Sarah Javed, and I am a second-year student at the University of Toronto. Growing up in the Crescent Town community as a second-generation immigrant, I witnessed many families around me facing challenges related to affording rent, food, and STEAM programming for youth.

Throughout the years, I have been actively involved in the community through Crescent Town Tenants Union, helping organize food drives, youth athletic programs, and other initiatives for youth. However, as many of these youth programs gradually stopped running in the community, I noticed there was a growing gap in free, accessible opportunities for young people.

Given my passion for STEAM, research, education, and mentorship, I wanted to help fill that void by creating free, hands-on learning opportunities for youth in Crescent Town. Through Crescent Town Youth Next, I hope to make STEAM education more accessible to youth in our community, while providing opportunities for them to learn new skills, explore their interests, and participate in activities they may not otherwise have access to.

Rayyan Javed

Vice President & Chief Operating Officer (COO) · Crescent Town Youth Next

Hi everyone! My name is Rayyan Javed, and I am a Grade 12 student in the Advanced Placement Program at Bloor Collegiate Institute. Over the years, like Sarah Javed, I have been involved in STEAM education and working with youth, from volunteering as a science camp leader at the Ontario Science Centre to working part-time at Kumon. As the Vice President and Chief Operating Officer of Crescent Town Youth Next, I work alongside Sarah to organize and deliver free educational programs for youth in our community. I hope to continue helping make STEAM education more accessible and engaging for young people in Crescent Town.

Supporting Our Volunteers

What Our Volunteers Gained

  • Community service hours: Earned hours that count towards the Ontario high school community service requirement.
  • Hands-on STEAM experience: Participated in the same activities as campers, including building electric cars, creating slime, making bouncy balls, and more. For many volunteers, these were also their first experiences with these activities.
  • Leadership experience: Gained practical experience supporting youth, assisting with activities, and taking on responsibilities throughout the camp.
  • Experience working with children: Developed communication, teamwork, and mentorship skills through working directly with campers.
  • Cash honorarium: Received a cash honorarium in recognition of their time and contributions to the summer camp.
  • Reference letters: Had the opportunity to request reference letters for future employment, scholarships, and academic or volunteer opportunities.
  • Volunteer recognition: Received recognition on Crescent Town Youth Next's website and Instagram for their contributions.
  • Food and snacks: Received food or snacks during camp sessions.
